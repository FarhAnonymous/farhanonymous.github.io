01 · Story

A model that works on one plantation should not be trusted blindly on the next.

I recently completed a research MSc in Aerospace Engineering (Research) at the University of Science Malaysia, supervised by Dr. Mohammad Nishat Akhtar. My thesis was approved by the USM Senate in October 2026, and the degree is conferred at the November 2026 convocation. It compares YOLO and transformer detectors for finding oil palm trees in UAV imagery.

Most of my time went into the practical side of the problem: building and labelling the dataset, comparing models, and getting the best one to run on a Raspberry Pi and other edge devices in the field. Along the way, I ran into a question I want to keep working on. What happens when a model that works well on one plantation is taken somewhere else, and how much should a user trust the count it gives?

If your group works on aerial or remote-sensing perception, I would be glad to hear from you.

02 · Research

What I work on, and what I want to work on next

01

Real-time Aerial Counting

Detecting small, densely packed trees in UAV imagery and counting them accurately. I compared YOLOv8, YOLOv11, and the transformer-based D-FINE on a large plantation dataset.

YOLOv8 on edge devices (2025) · YOLOv11 vs D-FINE (under review)

02

Edge Deployment

Making the detector small enough to run on a Raspberry Pi 4B with an Intel Neural Compute Stick, so it works in the field without a GPU or a network connection.

YOLOv8 on edge devices (2025)

03 · next

Reliable Counts Across Regions

Keeping a counter accurate when the region, species, sensor, or altitude changes, and attaching a measure of confidence to each count so people know which results to check by hand.

PhD proposal in preparation, available on request

05 · Projects

Things I built

MSc thesis · 2024–2026

UAV Oil Palm Detection and Counting

A full pipeline from drone image to count. I built a dataset of 6,080 DJI M30T images with 111,810 annotated trees across Penang, then compared YOLOv8, YOLOv11 and D-FINE. The pipeline reached 98.8% mAP@0.5 while staying real-time.

Python · PyTorch · YOLOv8 · YOLOv11 · D-FINE

MSc research

Palm Counting on a Raspberry Pi

Optimised the detector for a Raspberry Pi 4B with an Intel Neural Compute Stick using OpenVINO, so it can be used in plantations without a GPU or a network connection.

OpenVINO · ONNX · Raspberry Pi

Jan–May 2024

Cyclist Passing Distance from Video

A video pipeline that measures how closely motorists pass cyclists in naturalistic riding footage. The results fed into national bicycle safety research in Malaysia.

Python · OpenCV · YOLO · Statistics
