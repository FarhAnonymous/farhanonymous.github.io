About

I recently completed a research MSc in Aerospace Engineering at Universiti Sains Malaysia, supervised by Dr. Mohammad Nishat Akhtar. My thesis was approved by the USM senate in October 2026, and the degree is conferred at the November 2026 convocation. It compares YOLO and transformer detectors for finding oil palm trees in UAV imagery.

Most of my time has gone into the practical side of this problem: building and labelling the dataset, comparing models, and getting the best one to run on a Raspberry Pi in the field. Along the way I ran into a question I want to keep working on, which is what happens when a model that works well on one plantation is taken somewhere else, and how much a user should trust the count it gives.

I am applying for PhD positions in Australia and New Zealand. If your group works on aerial or remote-sensing perception, I would be glad to hear from you at er.farhan2000@gmail.com.

Research Interests

  • Aerial object detection and counting. Dense, small objects in UAV imagery, from plantations to forests.
  • Cross-domain generalisation. Models that keep working when the region, species, sensor or flight altitude changes.
  • Reliable predictions. Attaching a measure of confidence to a count, so that people know which results to check by hand.
  • Edge deployment. Real-time inference on low-power hardware for field use.

Currently preparing a PhD research proposal on trustworthy cross-domain aerial object counting. Happy to share it on request.

Selected Projects

UAV Oil Palm Detection and Counting

MSc thesis · 2024–2026

A full pipeline from drone flight to count. I built a dataset of 6,080 DJI M30T images with 111,810 annotated trees across Penang, then compared YOLOv8, YOLOv11 and the transformer-based D-FINE. The pipeline reached 98.8% mAP@0.5 while staying real-time.

Python · PyTorch · YOLOv8 · YOLOv11 · D-FINE

Palm Counting on a Raspberry Pi

Edge deployment · MSc research

Optimised the detector to run on a Raspberry Pi 4B with an Intel Neural Compute Stick using OpenVINO, so it can be used in plantations without a GPU or a network connection.

OpenVINO · ONNX · Raspberry Pi · Python

Cyclist Passing Distance from Video

School of Civil Engineering, USM · Jan–May 2024

Built a video pipeline that measures how closely motorists pass cyclists in naturalistic riding footage. The results fed into national bicycle safety research in Malaysia.

Python · OpenCV · YOLO · statistical analysis
