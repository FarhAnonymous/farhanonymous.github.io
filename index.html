About

I recently completed a research MSc in Aerospace Engineering at Universiti Sains Malaysia, supervised by Dr. Mohammad Nishat Akhtar. My thesis was approved by the USM senate in October 2026, and the degree is conferred at the November 2026 convocation. It compares YOLO and transformer detectors for finding oil palm trees in UAV imagery.

Most of my time went into the practical side of the problem: building and labelling the dataset, comparing models, and getting the best one to run on a Raspberry Pi in the field. Along the way I ran into a question I want to keep working on: what happens when a model that works well on one plantation is taken somewhere else, and how much a user should trust the count it gives.

If your group works on aerial or remote-sensing perception, I would be glad to hear from you at er.farhan2000@gmail.com.

Research Highlights

01

Real-time aerial counting

Detecting small, densely packed trees in UAV imagery and counting them accurately. I compared YOLOv8, YOLOv11 and the transformer-based D-FINE on a large plantation dataset.

Papers: YOLOv8 on edge devices (2025) · YOLOv11 vs D-FINE (under review)

02

Edge deployment

Making the detector small enough to run on a Raspberry Pi 4B with an Intel Neural Compute Stick, so it works in the field without a GPU or a network connection.

Papers: YOLOv8 on edge devices (2025)

03

Reliable counts across regions (next)

Keeping a counter accurate when the region, species, sensor or altitude changes, and attaching a measure of confidence to each count so people know which results to check by hand.

PhD proposal in preparation, available on request

Projects

  • UAV Oil Palm Detection and CountingMSc thesis · 2024–2026

    A full pipeline from drone image to count. I built a dataset of 6,080 DJI M30T images with 111,810 annotated trees across Penang, then compared YOLOv8, YOLOv11 and D-FINE. The pipeline reached 98.8% mAP@0.5 while staying real-time.

    Python · PyTorch · YOLOv8 · YOLOv11 · D-FINE

  • Palm Counting on a Raspberry PiMSc research

    Optimised the detector for a Raspberry Pi 4B with an Intel Neural Compute Stick using OpenVINO, so it can be used in plantations without a GPU or a network connection.

    OpenVINO · ONNX · Raspberry Pi
